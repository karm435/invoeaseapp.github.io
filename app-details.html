InvoEase app details

InvoEase is a simple invoice and quote maker for iPhone and iPad. It is built for freelancers, sole traders and small businesses who want to send professional invoices and quotes in minutes, without complicated accounting software.

Features

Invoices and quotes

Create invoices and quotes on the spot, then turn an accepted quote into an invoice with one tap.

Works offline

Working in rural areas or on a job site with no signal? Keep invoicing and sync when you are back online.

Flexible and localized

Whether you use VAT or GST, USD or EUR, you can adjust your invoice settings to match how you do business.

Reminders for overdue invoices

Remind clients about overdue invoices without writing awkward follow-up emails.

Multiple templates

Pick a template and customize it so your invoices look like your business.

iPhone and iPad

Use the bigger iPad screen when you need more space for invoicing and quoting.

Who it is for

Tradespeople, painters, cleaners, tutors, personal trainers, yoga instructors, massage therapists, craftspeople, event planners, makeup artists and any small business that needs to bill clients quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Is InvoEase available in multiple languages?

Yes. InvoEase is available in 47 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Portuguese. The app follows the language set on your iPhone or iPad.

Does InvoEase work offline?

Yes. You can create invoices and quotes without an internet connection and sync when you are back online.

Can I use VAT, GST and my own currency?

Yes. You can set VAT or GST and choose currencies such as USD or EUR in your invoice settings.

Does InvoEase work on iPad?

Yes. InvoEase runs on both iPhone and iPad.

Where is my data stored?

Your data stays on your device. InvoEase does not collect personal information.
