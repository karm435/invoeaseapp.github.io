Privacy Policy for Android

Last updated 30 September 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to Invoice generator: InvoEase for Android (com.karmaacademy.invoicely), provided by Karma Academy Pty Ltd. Contact us at support@invoeaseapp.com about privacy, access or deletion requests.

Your business records

Business profiles, client and supplier details, invoices, quotes, line items, notes, logos and signatures are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We do not upload these records to our servers. InvoEase has no account sign-in, cloud sync, advertising or analytics service.

Subscriptions and Google Play

Google Play processes trial and subscription purchases under Google’s privacy policy and Google Play terms. The app receives subscription product identifiers, purchase status and purchase tokens from Google Play to check access and acknowledge purchases. These checks communicate with Google Play. We do not receive your card number or payment credentials, and do not send your purchase tokens to a developer-operated billing server or RevenueCat. The app stores a local flag recording whether a subscription has previously been found; this flag alone cannot unlock access.

Photos, exports and sharing

The app accesses only images or documents you choose through Android’s picker. It creates PDFs locally. When you export or share a PDF, you choose its destination or recipient; the selected storage or sharing provider then handles that copy under its own terms. Invoice PDFs contain the business and client details you include.

Android backups

Android may back up or transfer your app data according to your device and Google account settings. We do not operate that backup service. Temporary demo data is excluded. Check your Android backup settings to manage backups and restored data.

Support and external links

If you choose to contact support, your email provider sends the message and we receive the information you include so we can respond. Do not send customer records or sensitive payment information unless needed for your request. Opening privacy, help or subscription-management links uses your browser or Google Play; those services apply their own privacy practices.

Retention, deletion and security

Records remain in your device’s app storage until you delete them, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Your exported copies and Android backups may remain; manage those separately with the relevant provider. We cannot retrieve your local records remotely. For support correspondence, contact us to request access or deletion, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Protect your device and exported documents with suitable access controls.

Changes

We will update this page when the Android app’s data practices change. The date below identifies this version of the policy.
